Digital History, Software Archaeology, & Preservation

The Paco1994 project stands as a stellar example of modern software archaeology, preserving the historical legacy of Spanish MS-DOS era development. Created in 1994 by Alcachofa Soft S.L. as a technology demo, this hybrid scene-graph title represents the crucial embryonic steps that directly inspired the iconic 1996 point-and-click adventure Drascula: The Vampire Strikes Back. Under formal preservation, this complete reverse-engineered codebase unlocks original resource formats (.ALD, .ALG, .ALS, .CMF) for future engines and platforms.
